# Legal, but not safe enough

What Australia's own pesticide tests found on fruit and veg: mostly tiny traces, and two legal pesticide uses (grapes, then berries) pulled once regulators saw how much of that fruit people eat.

Live: https://aussiedatagal.github.io/dirty-dozen/

## Data

- 25th Australian Total Diet Study (2019), Food Standards Australia New Zealand. Table A3.9 and Appendix 11. CC BY 4.0.
- Pesticides in berries survey (2026), NSW Food Authority. © State of New South Wales through the NSW Food Authority.
- Dimethoate suspension on berries (APVMA, 11 November 2025), via Fruitnet.
- 2026 Dirty Dozen and Clean Fifteen, Environmental Working Group, via Food Safety News (8 April 2026).

The files in `data/` were extracted from the published PDFs.
